Privacy policy

Replace this file (config/privacy.html) with your privacy policy. Template points for yourcve:

  • Controller: name and contact of the operator.
  • Data stored: email address, stacks (products and versions), notification channels (secrets encrypted), delivery log (180 days), sessions, passkeys (public keys only), API keys (hashed).
  • Purpose and legal basis: providing the service (Art. 6 (1) b GDPR).
  • Processors: email provider (SMTP), hosting provider; later with Plus: seven.io (SMS/voice/WhatsApp), Stripe (payments).
  • Bot protection: Anubis and ALTCHA run on this server, no third parties, no tracking.
  • Your rights: access, rectification, erasure (Settings → Delete account removes all data immediately), objection, complaint to a supervisory authority.
